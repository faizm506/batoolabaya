Our Story

A Kalupur label for everyday modest wear.

Made for daily wear

Batool Abaya trades in the Panchpatti market of Kalupur, Ahmedabad, as a retailer and wholesaler of abayas, burqas, dupattas, coats and nosepieces.

Our designs borrow the generous drape of Dubai and Istanbul and keep the detail restrained: fewer designs, better fabric, cleaner cuts.

The same piece is available to one customer or a hundred, and it ships anywhere in India.

Fabric first

We choose fabric for how it falls and how it wears over a full day.

Restrained detail

Trim is narrow and tonal. The cut carries the design.

Honest pricing

Prices are confirmed with you directly, whether you buy one piece or a lot.
